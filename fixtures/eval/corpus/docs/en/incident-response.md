# Incident response

## Severity levels

A SEV1 incident is a full outage of a customer-facing service. The on-call
engineer must acknowledge a SEV1 page within 5 minutes and open a bridge
call within 15 minutes.

## Postmortems

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident gets a blameless postmortem within 10 business
days. SEV3 incidents do not require a postmortem.
