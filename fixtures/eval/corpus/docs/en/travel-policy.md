# Travel and expenses policy

## Booking flights

Flights are booked through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is the
default for every trip. Premium economy is not reimbursed on flights shorter
than six hours; business class needs written approval from a vice president.

## Daily allowance

The daily allowance covers meals and local transport. Hotel costs are
reimbursed separately against the receipt.

| City      | Daily allowance (EUR) | Hotel cap per night (EUR) |
|-----------|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| Athens    | 55                    | 140                       |
| Berlin    | 62                    | 165                       |
| Paris     | 70                    | 190                       |
| Tokyo     | 85                    | 210                       |

## What is never reimbursed

Alcohol, minibar charges and traffic fines are never reimbursed, even with
a receipt. Lost receipts can be replaced by a signed statement only for
amounts below 25 EUR.

## Submitting a claim

Claims must be submitted within 45 days of the end of the trip. Claims
submitted later are rejected automatically by the expense system.
